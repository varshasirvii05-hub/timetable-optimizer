How it works

Two classes conflict when they share a student group or an instructor. Each class becomes a graph vertex, each conflict an edge, and greedy coloring gives adjacent vertices different colors, so each color becomes one time slot. Within a slot, classes are sorted by enrollment and each takes the smallest room that fits. Rooms needed equals the busiest slot.
